Refund Reconciler

A Chrome extension by MJUD for keeping track of refunds for items you returned, one item at a time, so you can see what is still unresolved.

Refund Reconciler is a beta that is not yet available to install. It is not in the Chrome Web Store.

Evidence you record, item by item

For each returned item, you keep four things apart, so a statement that a refund was issued is never mistaken for money you actually received.

Expected amount
The refund you expect for the item, or “unknown” if you do not know it yet.
Merchant reports
What the merchant says it issued. This is the merchant’s statement, not proof that you were paid.
Money received
Amounts you confirmed yourself after checking your own card or bank statement. Only these count as received.
Still unresolved
What you expected but have not confirmed receiving. It is a prompt to review your records, not a guarantee that money is owed.

What it does not do

  • Amazon US orders in US dollars only. Other stores, Amazon country sites and currencies are not supported.
  • No connection to your bank or to Amazon. It does not sign in anywhere, request refunds, contact merchants, move money or decide what you are owed.
  • You confirm money yourself. You record a receipt only after checking your own statement; the extension cannot see your accounts.
  • Capture is optional and limited. On amazon.com you can highlight one item’s refund line and approve a preview to record a merchant report. It reads only the text you highlight and the page address, understands only certain US-dollar wording, and has been tested only on example text, not on real Amazon refund pages. You can always type a merchant report in by hand instead.

Your records stay in your browser

Encrypted with your passphrase

Before you save anything, you choose a passphrase. Your records are stored in your Chrome profile, encrypted with a key that the passphrase protects. The passphrase itself is never stored.

No passphrase recovery

Nobody, including MJUD, can reset a forgotten passphrase. If you forget it, the only way forward is to erase the stored records and restore a backup you saved earlier.

Exports are plain text

Backup files, case summaries and text you copy are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe and share them only if you choose to.

Nothing is sent anywhere

The extension makes no network requests, has no account, sync, analytics or advertising, and never sends your records to MJUD or anyone else.

Before any of this, the extension explains how it handles your data and asks you to agree. The privacy policy has the details, including what capture reads and saves, how deletion works, and the limits of encryption.

The use of information received by Refund Reconciler adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Limited Use statement in the privacy policy.
