Support

Refund Reconciler is published by MJUD. You can email a question or report a problem.

Email MJUD

exiledeals@gmail.com

Please include:

  • a short description of the problem and what you were trying to do;
  • the extension’s version (shown on its card at chrome://extensions);
  • your Chrome version (Chrome menu → Help → About Google Chrome).

Please do not send your passphrase, order screenshots, bank or card records, backup files, case summaries, or text captured from a page. Support does not need them.

Emailing is separate from the extension

The extension never contacts MJUD and never sends your records anywhere. It has no feedback, crash-report or upload feature, and MJUD cannot see what you have stored. If you email, MJUD receives only your email address and what you choose to write or attach. See If you email MJUD in the privacy policy.

MJUD cannot give financial advice, contact Amazon or your bank for you, guarantee a refund, promise a reply time, or recover encrypted records.

Help you can find yourself

The in-product guide

Open the dashboard and choose How to use Refund Reconciler at the top. It walks through creating a case, recording merchant reports, confirming money received, and following up. Data and privacy, also at the top, shows how your data is handled.

When capture refuses your text

Capture works only on amazon.com and www.amazon.com, after you have agreed to the data practices and while your records are unlocked, and it recognises only certain refund wording. If it says Cannot propose a report from this selection, choose Discard (nothing is saved), then open the case in the dashboard and use Record merchant report to type the amount in yourself.

Unlocking and a forgotten passphrase

You need your passphrase again after Chrome restarts, after the extension is updated or reloaded, and after Lock now. There is no recovery service and no reset. If you have forgotten it, choose Erase stored data… and type ERASE to remove the stored records, then set a new passphrase and restore a backup you saved earlier. Erasing cannot be undone.

Backups

On the case list, Your data → Download all data (JSON)… saves a complete backup. Restore from JSON… brings it back into a dashboard that has no cases. Backups are not encrypted, so keep them safe. Removing the extension deletes its stored records, so download a backup first if you want to keep them.
