Refund Reconciler {{VERSION}}: test instructions for Chrome Web Store review

No account, login, payment or shared credentials are needed. Choose any
throwaway passphrase yourself. Use only made-up data; nothing in these steps
sends data anywhere.

1. Disclosure and setup
   - Pin Refund Reconciler from the puzzle-piece menu and click its toolbar
     icon. Before agreement the popup offers only "Open dashboard to review".
   - In the dashboard, read "How Refund Reconciler handles your data" and
     choose "Agree and continue". ("Not now" changes nothing and stores
     nothing. "Data and privacy" at the top shows the same text later.)
   - On "Protect your records", enter a throwaway passphrase of at least 12
     characters twice, tick the acknowledgment and choose "Protect my
     records".

2. Manual recording (synthetic data)
   - Choose "Create case", enter the order reference TEST-0001 and one item
     "Test item" with an expected refund of 35.00, then "Save case". Open
     the case.
   - "Record merchant report" of 35.00: the merchant report is shown, but no
     money is confirmed.
   - "Confirm money received" of 20.00: $20.00 confirmed against $35.00
     expected.
   - "Record recharge" of 5.00: the confirmed net amount drops to $15.00.
   - Optional: "Load synthetic demo" at the bottom of the case list adds two
     labelled fake cases; "Remove synthetic demo" deletes them.

3. Summary and backup
   - On the case, "Prepare case summary…" shows plain text to Copy or
     Download. Evidence details stay out unless included.
   - On the list, "Your data" > "Download all data (JSON)…" saves an
     unencrypted backup file.

4. Lock and unlock
   - Choose "Lock now". The records are hidden; the toolbar popup now offers
     only "Open dashboard to unlock".
   - Enter the same passphrase and choose "Unlock". The case is shown again.

5. Optional: selected-text capture refusing ordinary text
   - With the records unlocked, open https://www.amazon.com (no sign-in),
     highlight ordinary text such as a product name, click the toolbar icon
     and choose "Capture selected refund text".
   - Expected: "Cannot propose a report from this selection", with a reason.
     Choose "Discard"; nothing is saved.
   - On another https site, Capture reports that only pages on amazon.com
     or www.amazon.com are supported.

Limitation: the capture feature recognises only certain US-dollar refund
wording. It has been tested on example text only, not on real Amazon refund
pages; saving a captured report needs a real refund line for one item, which
review does not require. Manual entry covers the same records.
